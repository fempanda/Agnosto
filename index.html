Who we are

About Agnosto

Agnosto Media Productions is a home for creative expression, connecting artists across music, film, photography, and beyond to produce, collaborate, and be discovered.

The name draws on the fear of the unknown. The idea behind Agnosto is to give unfamiliar work room to exist and be heard.

Privacy, accessibility & terms

Privacy

This frontend preview does not transmit application or contact form submissions. Your content visibility preference is saved in your browser when local storage is available. Once the server and submissions are enabled, a complete privacy notice and data-handling procedures must be finalized before collecting personal information.

Accessibility

We aim to make Agnosto usable with a keyboard, screen reader, and mobile device. If you find an accessibility barrier, use the contact page when a working contact channel has been configured.

Copyright & credits

Creative work remains subject to the rights of its creators and other rights holders. Submission does not transfer ownership. Publication requires appropriate permissions, including for cover recordings and audiovisual uses.

Terms

Formal terms, guardian processes, and contact information must be approved before the public website begins accepting submissions. The current preview does not accept applications.
